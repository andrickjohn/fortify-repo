# Fortify: User Guide (District Administrators)

Welcome to Fortify, the vendor management platform designed to provide school districts with a competitive edge in procurement and contract management.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. Dashboard Overview
The **Executive Dashboard** provides a high-level view of your district's financial health:
- **Efficiency Score**: A proprietary metric tracking your district's procurement optimization status.
- **Value Delta**: A visual chart comparing contract spend trends against student enrollment data.
- **Urgent Renewals**: Real-time alerts for contracts expiring within the next 90 days.

### 2. Processing a Contract
To ingest a new vendor contract:
1. Navigate to the **Contracts** section in the sidebar.
2. Drag and drop your **PDF Purchase Order** or **Contract** into the upload zone.
3. Wait for the **Extraction Engine** to parse the data.
4. Review the extracted data (Vendor Name, PO Number, Total Value) and click **Approve & Save**.

### 3. Interpreting the Value Delta
If you see a **Value Delta Alert**, it means vendor spending is increasing while student enrollment is decreasing. This is a primary indicator for:
- Over-licensing (paying for seats transition students no longer exist).
- Need for contract renegotiation.

## 🛠 Features
- **Draggable Widgets**: Personalize your dashboard by dragging widgets to new positions.
- **Negotiation Insights**: AI-generated prompts for vendor meetings based on market benchmarks.
- **Multi-Tenant Security**: Your district's data is isolated and encrypted, accessible only to authorized personnel.
